THE COMMUNITY

A peninsula, not just a postcode.

Set across a 3-hectare beachfront site on Al Khan / Maryam Island, SEEFA is arranged as five contemporary residential buildings around a landscaped podium — designed with a Neo-Art Deco sensibility that softens the coastline's contemporary architecture. Every building connects directly to the beachfront and a shared public promenade — one of the only developments in the area with direct beach access, not a shared or public stretch a drive away.
